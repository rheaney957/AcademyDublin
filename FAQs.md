THE ACADEMY – FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

PROHIBITED ITEMSThe following items are not permitted inside the venue:
	•	Bottles (including unopened)
	•	Cans
	•	Food or outside beverages
	•	Selfie sticks
	•	Large umbrellas
	•	Professional cameras
	•	Video cameras
	•	Audio recording devices
	•	GoPros
	•	Large posters (A3 size or bigger)
	•	Large flags on poles
	•	Belt chains
	•	Laser pointers

BAGSBags no larger than 40cm x 40cm x 20cm are allowed.Large rucksacks, luggage, and oversized bags are not permitted.

AGE RESTRICTIONSAge restrictions vary by event.Please check Ticketmaster for event-specific details.

VENUE LOCATIONThe Academy57 Middle Abbey Street, Dublin 1(City Centre – easily accessible by public transport)

CONTACT INFORMATIONGeneral Enquiries: info@theacademydublin.comLost Property: lostproperty@theacademydublin.com

CAMERAS & RECORDING EQUIPMENTNo professional cameras, video cameras, or audio recording devices are allowed.

FOOD & DRINKOutside food and beverages, including unopened bottles, are not permitted.

ARRIVAL TIMESPlease check your ticket for door opening and show start times.Stage times are typically confirmed the day before or day of the show.For updates, also check our website, social media, or the event promoter’s page.

CLOAKROOM FACILITIESCloakrooms are available for storing bags and jackets.Cash and card are accepted.

LOST & FOUNDLost something at a show?Email us at lostproperty@theacademydublin.com or message us on social media.

EARPLUGSNeed ear protection?Ask any staff member — all bars stock complimentary earplugs.A Manager can also provide them free of charge.

NEED HELP DURING THE EVENT?A Duty Manager is on-site at every show to assist guests.Please speak to any staff member if you need help or would like to speak to a Manager.

ACCESSIBILITYOur venue is accessible for all guests.For assistance or access requirements, please email info@theacademydublin.com.

TICKETSTickets for all events are available at:www.theacademydublin.com
