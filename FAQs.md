THE ACADEMY – FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

PROHIBITED ITEMS The following items are not permitted inside the venue:
	•	Bottles (including unopened)
	•	Cans
	•	Food or outside beverages
	•	Selfie sticks
	•	Large umbrellas
	•	Professional cameras
	•	Video cameras
	•	Audio recording devices
	•	GoPros
	•	Large posters (A3 size or bigger)
	•	Large flags on poles
	•	Belt chains
	•	Laser pointers

BAGS Bags no larger than 40cm x 40cm x 20cm are allowed. Large rucksacks, luggage, and oversized bags are not permitted.

AGE RESTRICTIONS Age restrictions vary by event. Please check Ticketmaster for event-specific details.

VENUE LOCATION The Academy 57 Middle Abbey Street, Dublin 1 (City Centre – easily accessible by public transport)

CONTACT INFORMATION General Enquiries: info@theacademydublin.com Lost Property: lostproperty@theacademydublin.com

CAMERAS & RECORDING EQUIPMENT No professional cameras, video cameras, or audio recording devices are allowed.

FOOD & DRINK Outside food and beverages, including unopened bottles, are not permitted.

ARRIVAL TIMES Please check your ticket for door opening and show start times. Stage times are typically confirmed the day before or day of the show. For updates, also check our website, social media, or the event promoter’s page.

CLOAKROOM FACILITIES Cloakrooms are available for storing bags and jackets. Cash and card are accepted.

LOST & FOUND Lost something at a show? Email us at lostproperty@theacademydublin.com or message us on social media.

EARPLUGS Need ear protection? Ask any staff member — all bars stock complimentary earplugs. A Manager can also provide them free of charge.

NEED HELP DURING THE EVENT? A Duty Manager is on-site at every show to assist guests. Please speak to any staff member if you need help or would like to speak to a Manager.

ACCESSIBILITY Our venue is accessible for all guests. For assistance or access requirements, please email info@theacademydublin.com.

TICKETS Tickets for all events are available at: www.theacademydublin.com
